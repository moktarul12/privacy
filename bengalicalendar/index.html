Bengali English Calendar — Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 20, 2026 · Last updated: August 27, 2026

DromoMinds (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the Bengali English Calendar mobile application.

1. Introduction

Bengali English Calendar helps you view the Gregorian and Bengali calendars, festivals, holidays, and panchang information. The app does not require user registration or account creation.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Personal data

We do not collect personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number through a login or account system.

2.2 Device and app information

We may collect limited device or app information to keep the app working and improve it, such as:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Language and time zone preferences

2.3 App usage data

We may collect anonymous usage information to improve performance, for example:

  • Screen views and interactions
  • Calendar and display preferences stored on your device
  • Crash reports and error logs

3. How we use your data

  • Provide and maintain app functionality
  • Improve user experience and performance
  • Understand usage patterns and optimize features
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Help keep the app secure

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their marketing purposes. We may share limited data only when:

  • Service providers help us operate the app (for example, platform or analytics services)
  • Legal requirements require disclosure
  • A business transfer such as a merger or acquisition occurs

5. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, including secure transmission where applicable, access controls, and regular review of our practices.

6. Third-party services

The app may use standard platform and framework services, including:

  • Expo / React Native — app framework and updates
  • Google Play Services — Android notifications and updates
  • Apple services — iOS functionality where applicable

These providers have their own privacy policies.

7. Permissions

On Android, the app may request permissions such as:

  • Internet and network state — updates and online features
  • Notifications and alarms — festival or reminder alerts you enable
  • Vibration — optional notification feedback

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Opt out of optional analytics where offered
  • Contact your local data protection authority

9. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Data retention

We retain data only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. Anonymous usage data may be retained for up to 12 months for analytical purposes.

11. International data transfers

Data may be processed in countries other than your country of residence. We take steps to protect data in line with this policy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
